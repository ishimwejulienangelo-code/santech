    Assessment 1 — Embedded Systems & IoT¶

    Overview¶
    Title Embedded Systems & IoT — Simplest to Complex
    Date March 2026
    Status Completed

    Task Description¶

    I was tasked with studying and explaining Embedded Systems and the Internet of Things (IoT) — starting from the most basic concepts and building up to more complex real-world applications. This included understanding what embedded systems are, how they interact with the physical world through sensors and actuators, and how IoT connects these devices over a network to enable smart, automated solutions.

    My Approach¶

    1. I started by researching the fundamentals of embedded systems — understanding microcontrollers, basic input/output operations, and how hardware and software work together at a low level.
    2. I then studied IoT concepts, exploring how simple devices like temperature sensors and LEDs connect to more advanced systems such as smart home devices, industrial monitors, and network-connected controllers.
    3. Finally, I organized my findings from simplest to most complex — presenting a clear progression from a basic LED circuit all the way to a fully connected IoT network, demonstrating how each layer builds on the previous one.

    Reflection¶
    What went well I was able to clearly understand and explain the progression from simple embedded components to complex IoT systems. Breaking it down step by step made the topic much easier to grasp.
    What was challenging Understanding how different communication protocols (like MQTT and HTTP) are used in IoT systems was initially difficult, as there were many options with different use cases.
    What I'd improve I would spend more time doing hands-on practice with actual hardware or simulation tools to reinforce the theoretical knowledge with practical experience.
